## Scouting
Walk the field regularly, from emergence for maize and through fruiting for cocoa. Walk a W or zigzag path, stop at about five places, and check about 10 plants or trees at each, early in the morning. Note the share of plants or pods affected and the stage of the crop.

## Decide with evidence
Act when the share of damaged plants reaches a level where the cost of control is worth it. Early in the season the damage matters more than later. Write down what you see each week so you can compare.

## Order of action
First prevention: clean planting material, early planting, weed control, pruning, and sanitation. Then conserve natural enemies. Only then consider a registered product, chosen for the right pest and used as labelled.

## Good photos for the app
Take close, well-lit photos of the affected leaf, pod, or insect, with one clear subject. Retake if the image is blurry or dark. If the app is unsure, show the problem to an extension officer.
