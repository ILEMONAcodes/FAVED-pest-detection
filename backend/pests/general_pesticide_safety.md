## Before you buy
Spray only when scouting shows the problem is spreading and non-chemical steps are not enough. Use only products registered in Nigeria (NAFDAC) for your crop and pest, from a licensed dealer, in the original labelled container. Avoid unlabelled, repacked, or suspiciously cheap products; counterfeit chemicals are a known risk.

## When you spray
Read the label first: rate, timing, and the waiting period before harvest. Never mix products unless the label says you can. Wear gloves, long sleeves, boots, a mask, and eye cover. Do not spray against the wind, in rain, or near streams and wells. Keep children, animals, and bystanders away from the field.

## After you spray
Wash yourself and your clothes separately from other washing. Never reuse pesticide containers for food or water; follow local advice on safe disposal. Store products locked away from children, food, and animal feed. If someone feels sick after exposure, take them to a health centre and bring the label.

## Protecting helpers
Many insects kill pests for free. Broad-spectrum sprays kill them too and can make problems worse, so use selective or lower-hazard options where possible.

## Sources
