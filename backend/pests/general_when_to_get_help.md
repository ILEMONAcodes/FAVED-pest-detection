## Who to contact
Your local agricultural extension officer (state Agricultural Development Programme office) is the first contact for any crop problem. For cocoa, the Cocoa Research Institute of Nigeria (CRIN) in Ibadan is the national research body. For a suspected new or foreign pest or disease, the Nigeria Agricultural Quarantine Service (NAQS) should be told.

## Report immediately if
Cocoa pods show a cream or tan powdery crust with humped young pods. Cocoa trees show swollen shoots or red banding on young leaves. You see a pest that is new to your area, or a sudden large loss across many farms.

## What to bring or send
Clear photos of the whole plant and a close-up of the problem; the date and the place; the crop variety if known; and what you have already sprayed.

## If the app and your eyes disagree
The app is a guide, not a final diagnosis. If the advice does not match what you see, trust a person who can look at the plant.
